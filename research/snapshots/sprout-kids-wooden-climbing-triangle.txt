The Climbing Triangle is a simple resource that helps children develop their gross motor skills, helping them feel comfortable in who they are and their natural abilities to grow into their body space on their own terms. A triangle in your home can give your child enhanced gross motor development, increased spatial awareness, vestibular stimulation (for learning about balance), strengthening limbs, proprioceptive learning (physical self-awareness), providing open-ended play and more!
