Refer to all safety warnings provided on the product prior to use. Also, be sure to read the tie instructions included in the packaging carefully and follow carrying instructions that correspond with your baby’s weight. Suffocation Hazard Babies under 4 months can suffocate if their face is pressed against your body. Premature babies and those with breathing problems are at a higher risk. Check often to ensure your baby’s face is visible and away from your body. Make sure your baby’s chin is not resting on their chest. Reposition baby after nursing so their face is not pressed against your body. Do not use this product with a baby under 8 lbs without asking your healthcare provider. Fall Hazard Keep one hand on your baby while moving to prevent accidental falls. Bending, leaning, or tripping may cause the baby to fall. Only use with babies weighing between 8 and 25 lbs.
