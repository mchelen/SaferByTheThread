Booklet A Make mealtime moments easier and more confident with the Versa 3‑in‑1 High Chair--thoughtfully designed to support your little one from first bites through their toddler years and beyond. Built with everyday life in mind, this convertible high chair transitions from infant feeding seat to toddler chair to a comfortable adult seat, giving your family long‑lasting value and performance. Crafted with a durable aluminum frame, built-in wheels that make it easy to move around the house, and FSC-certified beechwood accents, the Versa blends modern style with sturdy construction that’s made to last. The easily wipeable seat pad and meal tray help simplify cleanup after messy meals. Adjust to every stage with effortless one‑hand height adjustments for the seat and footrest, and keep your child secure through every bite with a 5‑point safety harness and stable, non‑slip feet. Whether you’re serving first foods or family dinners, the Versa makes mealtime comfortable, safe, and beautifully integrated into your home.
