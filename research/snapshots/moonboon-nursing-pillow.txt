Colours Nature, Earth, Lunar Rock and Rose Cloud Care instructions The removable quilted cover can be washed on a normal cycle at 40°C. Do not tumble dry. To avoid shrinking when washing, we recommend steam ironing the nursing pillow cover when it’s still damp, pulling the fabric to restore its original shape. The quilted cover is made from organic cotton which is free from chemicals and pesticides. Washing before use is therefore not required.
