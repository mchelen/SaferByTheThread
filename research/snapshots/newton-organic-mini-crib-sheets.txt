Mini crib sheets measure 24" x 38" and fit a crib mattress up to 8" thick. We designed our Newton mini crib sheets to fit securely over the Newton Mini Crib Mattress, but they also fit other mini crib mattresses of similar dimensions. For standard-sized crib mattresses, try our standard crib sheets, which fit 52" x 28" mattresses, like Newton's Essential, Original, and Waterproof Crib Mattresses. What material is best for mini crib sheets? The best material for mini crib sheets is organic muslin cotton. This type of cotton is 100% breathable, 100% machine washable, and 100% comfortable. The breathable aspect is particularly important for two reasons: - It ensures that your baby will be able to breathe should they turn on their stomach - It helps them regulate their body temperature and stay warm at night For the best sleep experience for your baby, choose organic muslin cotton mini crib sheets! What is the GOTS certification for mini crib sheets? The Global Organic Textile Standard (or GOTS) certification is a set of standards that ensures crib sheets are: - Made from natural fibers - Free of harmful chemicals - Free of toxic dyes - Super soft - Durable - Comfortable - Hypoallergenic - 100% breathable Mini crib sheets — or any sheets, for that matter — that aren’t GOTS certified may be made with toxic synthetic chemicals that can leach out and affect your baby’s health. How do I know if my mini crib sheet fits properly? A properly fitted mini crib sheet should: - Be pulled taut over the mattress with no loose fabric - Have corners that fit securely around the mattress - Not come loose if your baby moves while they sleep When putting a sheet on your baby’s mattress for the first time, inspect the flat surface where your little one will sleep. If you see loose material that creates folds or the corners look like they might come untucked, the sheet may not be the right size. Similarly, if you move your hand back and forth on the sheet after it’s on the mattress and it comes loose, you may need to try a different size. How many mini crib sheets should I have on hand? A good rule of thumb is to have three or four mini crib sheets on hand. Why? Because accidents will happen — and often! You’ll only need one mini crib sheet on the mattress at a time, but your baby may have a diaper blowout or throw up in their crib. You don’t want to be doing laundry in the middle of the night when that happens, so having sheets on hand is always a good idea. How often should I change crib sheets? Change your baby’s mini crib sheet about every three days (or twice a week). You can certainly change it more often if you like, but twice a week helps prevent dead skin, hair, and dust from accumulating where your baby sleeps and attracting bacteria and pests.
