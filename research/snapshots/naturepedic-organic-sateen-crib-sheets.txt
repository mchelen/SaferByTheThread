Sateen is a cotton fabric woven using a four-over-one-under pattern, which puts more yarn on the surface and creates a smooth, slightly lustrous finish. It's softer and more drapey than a standard percale weave, with a buttery feel that's particularly gentle against delicate skin. What's the difference between the sateen and muslin crib sheets? Both are made from GOTS-certified organic cotton, but they have a very different feel. Sateen crib sheets are smooth, soft, and slightly silky — closer to a classic sheet texture. Muslin crib sheets are gauzy and barely-there, with a looser weave that makes them more breathable. Sateen is a great choice if you want a softer, more polished look and feel. Muslin is better if temperature regulation is your top priority, since its open weave allows more airflow. Will these sheets fit my crib mattress? These sheets come in several options. The standard size is designed to fit standard crib mattresses (28" x 52") up to 6 inches deep. They fit all Naturepedic standard crib mattresses and most standard crib mattresses on the market. The two oval options are designed specifically for the Stokke Sleepi V2 and V3 crib models. Please note that neither the standard nor oval options are designed for use with a mini crib. Are these crib sheets free of harmful chemicals? Yes. These sheets are GOTS-certified , which means both the organic cotton and the manufacturing process meet strict non-toxic standards — no GOTS-prohibited synthetic dyes, formaldehyde-based finishes, or chemical fabric treatments. They're also MADE SAFE® certified, meaning they've been screened against over 15,000 potentially harmful substances. How many crib sheets do I need? These sheets are sold individually, so you can stock up on the quantity that works for your routine. We recommend having at least two or three on hand — one on the crib, one in the wash, and one ready to go for middle-of-the-night changes. Can I use these sheets with a mattress protector? Yes — and we recommend it. Layer a Waterproof Organic Cotton Jersey Crib Mattress Protector between the mattress and the sheet for protection against spills and accidents. How do I wash sateen crib sheets? Machine wash warm on a gentle cycle with a mild detergent. Tumble dry on low heat or line dry. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners, which can break down the fibers. The sateen weave holds up well to regular washing and maintains its smooth finish over time.
