A convertible crib is the same size as a standard crib, but it can be adapted to suit the needs of your baby as they grow. A baby typically sleeps in a crib until they’re around three years old, but with a convertible crib, you can transform it instead of tossing it! It usually starts as a standard crib with four fixed sides, keeping your baby safe and secure, and then when they outgrow it, you can easily remove one or more of the bed rails and add a guardrail to turn it into a toddler bed. Some convertible cribs, like the Newton Galileo 3-in-1 Convertible Crib, can be transformed a second time into a “big kid” bed by removing more rails and leaving one side completely open. Not only does a convertible crib save you time and money, but it's also a great choice if you want to save space in your baby’s bedroom. What is a 3-in-1 crib? A 3-in-1 crib, also known as a convertible crib, is a piece of furniture that can be converted from a crib to a toddler bed to a daybed, or “big kid” bed. It grows with your child, eliminating the need for a new bed every couple of years and saving time, money, and space. What size is a convertible crib? A convertible crib is about the same size as a standard crib — around 28 inches wide by 52 inches long. The size doesn’t change when the crib is converted into a toddler or twin bed. Instead, the rails or sides can be moved or removed. When should I switch my child from a crib to a toddler bed? Switch from a crib to a toddler bed when your little one grows to 35 inches tall or starts trying to climb out of the crib on their own (even if they haven’t reached the height limit yet). At either of those points, it’s safe to switch to a toddler bed. Keep in mind that this transition is a big milestone! By switching to a toddler bed, you move your little one closer to the ground and give them much more freedom. And with a convertible crib, you can help them feel like a "big kid" while still using the bed they already know and love. Is it hard to turn the Newton Galileo Convertible Crib into a toddler bed? Not at all! Many cribs require you to buy extra parts, but Newton Convertible Cribs come with the conversion kits included. To switch it over, take out the mattress and unscrew one side of the crib. Make sure the mattress support is at its lowest level for safety, and attach the toddler rail. Place the mattress back on the frame (making sure it fits snugly with no gaps), put sheets on the bed, and you’re good to go. We’ve designed the process to be simple and stress-free for busy parents. You only need basic tools to make the change, allowing you to focus on celebrating your toddler’s big milestone rather than struggling with complicated furniture instructions. How high should the mattress be for my newborn? When you first bring your baby home, you’ll want to use the highest mattress setting (the "Newborn" or bassinet level). This makes it much easier for you to lay your little one down without straining your back. When your baby starts to sit up or pull themselves up to a standing position, move the mattress to a lower setting (the crib level) to prevent them from accidentally tumbling out. Lowering the mattress takes just a few minutes but provides huge peace of mind and ensures that your baby stays securely inside the rails. This is especially important as they become more active and start to reach for things around them. How long will my child be able to use a Newton crib? Our cribs are designed to grow right along with your baby! Because they are fully convertible, you can use them from the day you bring your newborn home all the way through their toddler years and beyond. With three different height settings, you can easily adjust the crib as your little one gets bigger and more mobile. This longevity means you won’t have to shop for a new bed every time your child hits a growth spurt. Our 3-in-1 design eventually transforms into a daybed, giving your "big kid" a familiar place to sleep. It’s a smart, long-term investment that saves you time, money, and nursery space.
