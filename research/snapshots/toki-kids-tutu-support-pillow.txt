Soft support for pregnancy, nursing, and tummy time Size: 40" long x 5" wide. Recommended ages 0+. Materials Accessories: All support pillows are packaged in an organic cotton drawstring duster bag. Pillow insert: Certified organic 100% latex foam scraps. Our foam is made from rubber tree sap, using the energy efficient Dunlop production process. The foam has a supportive and springy feel, with small air pockets that make it completely breathable. Cover: 100% certified organic cotton fabric. Safety warning: Check if anyone in the household has allergies to latex in any form. Natural Foam Because our latex foam is made without any synthetic chemicals, the final product often has slight variations in appearance that do not affect quality, performance, or durability in any way. It is common for small tears and bubbles to form during the baking process, and any discolorations you may notice are the residuals of burnt latex sap. These imperfections are the natural result of clean manufacturing, and are undetectable once the foam is inserted into the support pillow cover. Care Cover: 100% organic cotton cover is not pre-shrunk, but is cut slightly larger to allow for some shrinkage. However, we still recommend washing and drying with no heat. If your cover already fits a bit snug, air dry only is recommended. If your cover fits a bit loose on the foam, low heat is suggested. After the first wash, the cover should fit perfectly snug and stretch out after just a few plays. Pillow insert: Spot clean natural latex foam with mild soap and water. If the natural foam is put into direct sunlight or exposed to air for several weeks, it will begin to dry out and become brittle. If your natural foam becomes wet, remove the cover and let the components dry separately in a room. Testing and Certification GOTS certified organic cotton. GOLS certified organic foam insert. Tested for harmful substances including phthalates, PFAs, BPA and heavy metals. Safety compliance reports can be found here and here. A portion of our proceeds goes to Every Mother Counts: Providing Maternity Care Worldwide Learn More Support Pillow FAQ How sturdy is the support pillow? Our support pillows are made from the same organic latex foam that is in our play mats. The springy, responsive foam is the perfect balance between soft and sturdy to support you or your little ones. Does it fold easily? The latex foam core is relatively dense, so while it bends very easily, we do not recommend folding it. Is the cover removable? Yes, there is a hidden zipper about three-quarters of the way along one side to remove the pillow insert. You can wash the cover or switch it out with another. Is the pillow intended for sleep or play? Because the pillow is such a versatile item, we recommend it for many uses but not for sleep. It is best used as pregnancy and nursing support, or for little ones who need a tummy time prop, sitting support, a play barrier, or a cuddle pillow. What are the care instructions? The cover is not pre-shrunk, so we recommend washing and drying with no heat. Spot clean the natural latex foam insert with mild soap and water. If your natural foam becomes wet, remove the cover and let the components air dry separately.
