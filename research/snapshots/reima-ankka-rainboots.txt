Classic cotton-lined rubber wellies for all ages! The Reima Ankka Rainboots combine vegan-friendly, fully waterproof construction with a zero heel drop design that supports natural foot development. The wide fit gives little toes plenty of room to move, while the flexible rubber outsole handles any adventure—from puddle jumping to muddy trails. Age-tailored shaft heights ensure the perfect fit, and the easy pull-on design means kids can gear up independently. Built for durability and all-weather exploration, these boots keep feet dry and comfortable so your child can play with confidence. As always, these shoes fulfill the requirements to be truly barefoot shoes- including having a wide natural foot shaped toe box, flexible soles for proper foot movement and zero drop from toe to heel.
