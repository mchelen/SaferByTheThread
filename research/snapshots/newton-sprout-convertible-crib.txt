What is important when looking for a crib? When searching for a crib, ensure that it meets (and, hopefully, exceeds) all safety standards by looking for the Juvenile Products Manufacturers Association (JPMA) certification and Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) seal. In addition, the crib slats should be no wider than two and three-eighths inches apart. If the slats are any farther apart, your baby could get a body part stuck in-between them. Stick with a crib that’s brand new and has all of its parts intact, and register your crib right away. If the manufacturer recalls your baby’s crib for any reason, they’ll notify you and offer a replacement, a warning or a fix.
