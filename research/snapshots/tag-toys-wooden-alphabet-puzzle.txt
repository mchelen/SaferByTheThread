Cart Description Dimensions Materials Shipping Info FAQ The Wooden Alphabet Puzzle by TAG Toys is a hands on Montessori material designed to support early literacy development before writing begins. Through tactile exploration, children build familiarity with letters, sounds, and simple word formation while strengthening fine motor skills. This puzzle features oversized, raised wooden letters in bright primary colors that are easy for small hands to grasp and place. The bold shapes and colors support visual discrimination and letter recognition, encouraging independent exploration and repetition. A built in spelling tray allows children to stand letters upright and form words by copying the included word cards. This interactive feature introduces early spelling in a concrete, approachable way while reinforcing letter sounds and sequencing. Made with a durable, high impact laminate surface, the puzzle is built for long lasting use in both home and classroom settings. 📚 Key Benefits ✔ Develops fine motor control and hand eye coordination ✔ Teaches letter names, sounds, and alphabetical order ✔ Enhances visual discrimination through shape and color ✔ Introduces early spelling with a hands on word tray 🌟 Proudly made in the USA 🏆 Oppenheim Toy Portfolio Award 🏆 KDFW TV Toy Test Award 🏆 Manhattan Mothers and Others Award 🎯 Recommended for ages 3 to 4 23"(L) x 13"(W) x 2.5"(H) Weight is 6.5 lbs The puzzle is made of high impact laminate surface which is durable.
