Our Beeswax Crayons Junior are chunkier crayons, providing a comfortable grip for toddlers ages 1 to 3 years old. If your child is 3 or older, our Beeswax Crayons Classic may be a better fit. The Classic crayons are thinner, standard-size crayons ideal for more detailed drawing and coloring, as well as children who are learning or proficient at using a pincer grasp. Are beeswax crayons non-toxic? Yes, our Beeswax Crayons are non-toxic and perfectly safe for children over the age of 1. They are made of 100% beeswax, a safe and natural material. Additionally, we use natural, organic pigments to create vibrant, non-toxic colors. We do recommend always using our crayons with supervision. Although all materials are safe for children, they pose a choking hazard if broken into smaller pieces. Are crayons made out of beeswax? Most standard wax crayons are made of paraffin. Piccalio’s Beeswax Crayons in both the Junior and Classic styles are made of 100% natural virgin beeswax. Are beeswax crayons safe for toddlers? Yes, Beeswax Crayons are safe for toddlers as long as they are being adequately supervised by an adult. Beeswax crayons are safe and non-toxic, even if accidentally ingested. However, keep in mind they may pose a choking hazard if broken into smaller pieces. What kind of crayons are safe for a 2-year-old? Most crayons are safe for a 2-year-old when used with supervision. However, natural crayons made of beeswax tend to be safer in case your child accidentally ingests them. Are there non-toxic crayons? Yes, many crayons are labeled non-toxic because they are not dangerous if accidentally ingested. Beeswax crayons and other natural crayons are often considered a superior choice for non-toxic crayons because they are typically made with natural pigments instead of synthetic ones. Standard non-toxic crayons are usually made of paraffin, a petroleum-based material, and synthetic pigments.
