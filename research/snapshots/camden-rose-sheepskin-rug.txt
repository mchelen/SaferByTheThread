Our sheepskins are sourced from US sheep and are tanned in the US using tree bark extracts from Argentina. This is one of the oldest methods of tanning which takes a bit longer (it takes three days to absorb) but is necessary for a natural product free of any heavy metals (the current standard is to tan skins using the chrome method which uses heavy metals).
