Shipping Info FAQ The RAD Two Step Stool is the natural next step after the Toddler Tower , designed for growing children who are ready for greater independence. It offers a sturdy, stable way to reach sinks, counters, and shelves throughout the home while maintaining the craftsmanship and quality RAD is known for. Whether in the kitchen, bathroom, or anywhere a child needs a boost, this two step design supports practical life skills and everyday participation. Children can confidently wash hands, brush teeth, help prepare meals, and access what they need on their own. Thoughtfully sized and solid underfoot, the stool weighs 10 pounds, heavy enough to feel secure yet manageable for children to move independently. Built in handles at the top allow little ones to carry it from room to room with ease. The top step sits at 12 inches high, providing comfortable access without the full enclosure of the Toddler Tower, making it a natural graduation piece as balance and confidence develop. Handmade in our shop from premium Baltic birch wood, the Two Step Stool reflects the same sturdy construction, smooth finish, and attention to detail as the RAD Toddler Tower. It easily supports up to 250 pounds, making it just as useful for adults as it is for children.
