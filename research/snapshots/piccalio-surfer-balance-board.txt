A wooden balance board is ideal for children ages 12 months and up. Our kids balance board has a weight limit of 485 pounds, meaning children can enjoy it for many years. Even adults may enjoy a balance board. Are balance boards good for kids? Yes, balance boards are good for kids because they encourage active play and creativity. A kids balance board can spark hours of imaginative play while also helping children build muscles and coordination. Children crave movement, including <a href="https://piccalio.com/blogs/grow/why-kids-love-spinning-in-circles-15" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">spinning and rocking</a> because they’re developing important systems in the body that manage balance and spatial awareness. A balance board is the perfect, safe outlet for these activities, supporting development. What is the difference between a wobble board and a balance board? A wobble board is a board with a half sphere on the bottom that can move in any direction while a balance board typically allows more of a back and forth movement. A balance board is typically a longer, curved board, or a board with a cylinder attached to the bottom. What safety testing does Piccalio follow? Playing should be fun. But also safe. Piccalio toys and furniture all have a children’s product certificate. They’re non-toxic, FSC certified wood, and compliant with ASTM, Prop65, and the FDA too. We never use harmful chemicals or substances in the making or manufacturing of our products. What material and sealant is used for the Surfer Balance Board? The Surfer Balance Board is made from FSC Certified (sustainably harvested) European Beech plywood. We use a 0-VOC water-based finish that is formaldehyde and lead free. What is the weight limit for the Surfer Balance Board? The weight limit is up to 485 lbs (220 kg). How does a Balance Board help develop gross motor skills? A wooden Balance Board encourages children to shift their weight, stabilize their core, and adjust their body position while playing. These small movements help strengthen muscles used for balance, coordination, and overall body control. Over time, activities like rocking, standing, or stepping on the board can support the development of important gross motor skills. Movement and gross motor development is essential for your child’s health. Here’s what you should know about exercise and kids. What are different ways kids can play with a Balance Board? Balance Boards are open-ended toys that can be used in many creative ways. Children may use the board for rocking, balancing, sliding, building bridges, or turning it into imaginative play structures like tunnels or ramps. Because there is no single “correct” way to use it, kids naturally explore movement and creativity at their own pace. One fun way to use a Balance Board is in an obstacle course. Find fun indoor obstacle course ideas here. What developmental stage benefits most from Balance Board play? Balance Boards are often introduced once children are confidently standing and beginning to explore movement and balance. For most children, this is after they’re 1-year-old. As children grow, the board continues to support new challenges—from simple rocking and stepping to imaginative play structures and more advanced balance activities. Young children are also developing their vestibular and proprioceptive systems, which is supported by movements like rocking and spinning.
