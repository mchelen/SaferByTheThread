Learn More We're here to help Personal Support, Every Step of the Way Read All FAQs At what age can a child use an organic latex pillow? Pillow use is recommended from age 2 and up, at the parent’s discretion. The Obasan organic latex toddler pillow is designed with a sealed, non-accessible core. If you are unsure whether your child is ready to use a pillow, we recommend consulting your pediatrician or healthcare specialist. Is this organic latex pillow safe for children? Yes. This pillow was designed specifically for children, featuring: A fixed, non-adjustable height A sealed core that cannot be accessed Materials free from chemical flame retardants and VOCs It provides a stable, safe, and breathable sleep environment. Is an organic latex pillow suitable for children with allergies? Yes. Organic latex is inherently hypoallergenic and resistant to dust mites, bacteria, and mold. It requires no chemical treatments, making it a reassuring choice for children with sensitivities or respiratory allergies. Organic latex is not synthetic foam,it comes from rubber tree sap and does not emit harmful off-gassing. Can you wash an organic latex toddler pillow? Yes, with care. For spot cleaning, use a damp cloth and mild soap. For deep cleaning, the shredded latex core can be washed in cold water, then air dried completely before reuse. Avoid heat, hot water, and machine drying at all costs, heat damages organic latex. Does an organic latex pillow sleep hot? No. organic latex has an aerated structure that promotes air circulation and limits heat buildup. It helps maintain a comfortable sleep surface even for children who tend to run warm at night. Where is the Obasan organic latex toddler pillow made? Every pillow is handcrafted in Canada using certified organic latex and organic cotton. This local manufacturing ensures full traceability, high quality standards, and a responsible approach to children's bedding.
