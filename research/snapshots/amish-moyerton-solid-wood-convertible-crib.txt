Every piece is built to order by a small Amish shop, so lead times vary by builder — the estimated completion and delivery window is shown above the Add to Cart button. Do I need tools to assemble my furniture? Cribs and conversion kits are assembled at home and aren’t part of White-Glove setup. The crib goes together with Allen-head bolts; the hardware, an Allen wrench and step-by-step instructions are included. The toddler bed and daybed conversions use the same kind of bolts, and the full-size bed rails attach to the headboard and footboard with hex bolts and heavy-duty brackets. Dressers, chests and nightstands can still be delivered White-Glove. Do you offer custom sizes or finishes? Yes. Chat with us or call (717) 768-7642 and our designers will quote it. What if I change my mind after ordering? You can cancel for a full refund within 72 hours of placing your order. After that, requests are reviewed individually — once the woodworker has sourced materials or begun building, a 25% restocking fee applies to approved cancellations. Custom-built items cannot be returned once delivered, so please inspect your furniture on arrival and report any damage within 48 hours. How do I care for my nursery furniture? Wood responds to its environment. For best results keep your furniture between 65 and 75°F with 35–40% relative humidity, and away from air-conditioning vents, fireplaces, radiators, space heaters, humidifiers and dehumidifiers. Keep pieces out of direct sunlight, which can fade or darken colors; white finishes naturally yellow over time, and sunlight speeds this up. Use coasters, protect tops from hot or cold items, and lift (don’t drag) furniture when moving it. To clean, wipe with a soft cloth lightly dampened in water with a little mild dishwashing liquid, then dry completely. Polish only two or three times a year, and don’t wax. How long does your nursery furniture take to build? Every piece is built to order by a small Amish shop in Lancaster County, PA. Most pieces take about 16–18 weeks from order to completion.
