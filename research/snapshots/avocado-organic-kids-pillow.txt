When your child outgrows the Organic Toddler Pillow, the Avocado Organic Kids Pillow is the next step. It's a full-size pillow — 19" × 25", the same footprint as our adult Green Pillow — tuned with a lower, gentler loft made for your growing child. It's a GOTS-certified organic pillow (CU863637), certified as a finished product from farm to finished pillow — not just one material inside it — and crafted with an organic cotton shell, shredded organic latex, and organic kapok fiber. No synthetic microfiber, polyester, PLA (plant-based plastic), or polyamide fibers are used. Designed for kids ages 3 and up, it's intentionally sewn shut—no zippers, no removable fill—for safer, simpler sleep. It also meets the strictest non-toxic certifications, including OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 Class I — the most restrictive tier, designed for products used by babies and children — and MADE SAFE®.
