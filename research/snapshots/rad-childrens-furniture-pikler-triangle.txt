Add to Cart Description Dimensions Materials Shipping Info FAQ The RAD Pikler® Triangle is a foundational gross motor tool designed to support balance, coordination, strength, and confidence in young children. It is the only Pikler Triangle certified by the Pikler Organization in the United States , developed in alignment with the pedagogical principles of Dr. Emmi Pikler to encourage safe, self directed movement and exploration. Handmade in the USA with RAD’s signature attention to detail and quality of materials, this climbing triangle provides a sturdy, inviting structure for children learning to pull to stand, climb, and navigate space with their bodies. Its intentional design supports both independent movement and shared play, allowing children to climb inside, collaborate with peers, and invent new ways to engage with the structure. The triangle features an automatic locking mechanism to keep it securely open during use and folds flat to under 5 inches for easy storage when not in use. Pair it with the Reversible Climbing Ramp to expand climbing, sliding, and open ended play possibilities. 👶 Age and Developmental Use The Pikler Triangle is best suited for children from about 9 months through 6 years . The most significant developmental benefits typically occur between 9 months and 30 months , when children are rapidly building balance, coordination, strength, and confidence through movement. As children grow, the triangle continues to offer value through more advanced climbing challenges, imaginative play, and collaborative exploration, making it a long lasting addition to any play space. 🪵 Key Features ✔ The only Pikler Triangle certified by the Pikler Organization in the United States ✔ Designed according to Dr. Emmi Pikler’s developmental principles ✔ Automatic lock keeps the triangle safely open during use ✔ Folds flat to under 5 inches for easy storage ✔ 3.5 inch spacing between rungs ✔ Supports at least 100 lbs per child ✔ Compatible with the Reversible Climbing Ramp ✔ Handmade with care in the USA 🛠 Assembly and Shipping • Ships unassembled • Tools included for easy assembly 🏠 Use and Care • Designed for indoor use • May be used outdoors for short periods, but prolonged exposure to moisture and UV rays is not recommended ⚠️ Safety Note Although our products are crafted to encourage exploration and free form play, children should always be supervised during use to ensure safe play. 28"(L) x 35"(W) x 29"(H) [when open] 28"(L) x 4.75"(W) x 33.5"(H) [when folded] Weight is 13 lbs Crafted from premium 13-ply European birch plywood made without urea-formaldehyde adhesives for exceptional durability and a beautiful natural wood appearance.
