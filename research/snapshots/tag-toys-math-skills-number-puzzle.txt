Materials Shipping Info FAQ The Montessori Math Skills Number Puzzle by TAG Toys is a complete, hands-on math readiness center designed to build foundational number skills through tactile learning. This thoughtfully designed set combines a wooden number puzzle, 20 counting beads, and a reversible chalkboard to help children understand numbers in both concrete and symbolic ways. Children begin by matching numbers and counting beads to build quantity awareness and number recognition. As skills progress, the reversible chalkboard introduces connect-the-dot number tracing and basic math symbols, allowing kids to practice writing numbers and explore simple addition and subtraction. This step-by-step approach supports early problem-solving by connecting physical objects with visual and symbolic math tools. Ideal for both independent exploration and guided learning, this award-winning puzzle encourages confidence in early numeracy while strengthening fine motor skills and logical thinking. Its multi-sensory design helps math concepts stick, laying a strong foundation for future learning. 🔢 Key Benefits ✔ Teaches number recognition, naming, and writing ✔ Builds quantity awareness with hands-on counting beads ✔ Reinforces sequencing and basic math functions ✔ Supports fine motor development and early logic 🌟 Proudly made in the USA 🎯 Recommended for ages 4 to 6 🏆 Award: KDFW-TV Fox 4 Dallas Toy Test, Top 20 Toys ⚠️ Choking hazard.
