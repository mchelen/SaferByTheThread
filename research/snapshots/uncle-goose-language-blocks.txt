Made from sustainable Michigan basswood, these wooden blocks invite children to build, stack, sort, and arrange while exploring letters, symbols, and illustrations. Open-ended building tools serve many purposes in the classroom. As children create structures and explore the designs on each block, they practice fine motor skills, spatial thinking, and early literacy. Kodo’s curated collection of Uncle Goose Language Blocks gives educators and families a hands-on way to celebrate familiar languages and introduce new ones. Available in German, French, Dutch, Hebrew, Portuguese, Sign Language, Hindi, Greek, Spanish, Korean, Japanese, Italian, Cherokee, Arabic, Vietnamese, Polish, Persian, Norwegian, Chinese, Swahili, and Ukrainian. Choose a language to support your classroom community, connect with a child’s home language, or invite curiosity about how people communicate around the world.
