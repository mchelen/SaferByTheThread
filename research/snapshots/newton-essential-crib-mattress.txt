Choosing a breathable mattress is important for your baby’s safety and comfort. Breathable mattresses have more air flow. This extra airflow also means reduced risk of suffocation‡. If your baby flips over onto their stomach while sleeping and is unable to flip back, they can breathe right through the mattress. Breathable mattresses are also antimicrobial and dust-mite-resistant. ‡Based on independent testing. What should I look for in a crib mattress? When choosing a crib mattress, look for one that’s firm, breathable, fits snugly in your chosen crib, and features safety certifications like UL GREENGUARD Gold. There are many types of mattresses available, ranging from foam and innerspring models to hybrid mattresses made of several different materials. There are benefits to each type, depending on your priorities. Different crib mattresses offer different features as well. For instance, some mattresses are lightweight, which can make it easier to change the sheets. Others are waterproof, great for spit-ups and accidents, and still others are dual-sided, providing a firm side for babies and a slightly softer side for toddlers. How can I be sure my crib mattress is safe? A safe crib mattress must be firm, flat, and fit snugly in your crib. To test firmness, push down on the mattress and ensure it snaps back up immediately and doesn’t leave an indentation. As for snugness, you shouldn’t be able to fit more than two fingers between any side of the crib and the mattress. It’s important to purchase your mattress new, as used mattresses may lose their firmness or have other unknown damage that could be dangerous. On top of being well-fitted and firm, choose a mattress that is breathable so that you reduce the risk of suffocation‡ and is UL GREENGUARD Gold Certified. ‡Based on independent testing. What is UL GREENGUARD Gold certification? GREENGUARD Certified products are certified to GREENGUARD standards for low chemical emissions into indoor air during product usage. (For more information, visit ul.com/gg). When it comes to choosing nursery products for your baby, looking for UL GREENGUARD Gold Certified products can help you breathe easy, knowing you’re making better choices for your little one. What size is the Newton Essential Crib Mattress? Standard crib mattress sizes are regulated to meet a minimum sizing of 27 1/4" x 51 5/8" and not thicker than 6 inches. Standard crib sizes, while also regulated, can vary a bit based on construction. If a crib is labeled as a standard crib, Newton Crib Mattresses will fit nice and snugly inside.
