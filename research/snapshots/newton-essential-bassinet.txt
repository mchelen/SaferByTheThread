The Newton Essential Bassinet features a Wovenaire® breathe-thru mattress, engineered for maximum airflow and safety. Made without foam, glue, latex, or springs, its patented Wovenaire® core is 90% air and 10% food-grade polymer, delivering firm, breathable support. At 1.5” deep, this mattress is as thick as safety regulations allow, ensuring it meets the strictest industry standards for bassinets. This depth is carefully designed to provide both comfort and compliance, reducing suffocation risk while maintaining the highest level of breathability and support for your baby. What is a stand-alone bassinet? A stand-alone bassinet is a safe, cozy sleep space designed just for newborns. Unlike bedside bassinets that attach to your bed, a stand-alone bassinet is a free-standing piece of furniture that gives your baby their own dedicated sleep surface—while still keeping them close. For safe sleep, always place it at least a foot away from your bed. What are the main benefits of the Essential Bassinet? The Newton Essential Bassinet is perfect for parents looking for a breathable, lightweight, and travel-friendly sleep solution that offers maximum safety and convenience. Here’s why someone might choose it over a bedside sleeper: Bigger Sleep Space – The Essential Bassinet has the largest-in-class sleep surface (35” x 19”), allowing for longer use up to 6 months compared to many bedside sleepers. 360° Visibility & Airflow – Fully breathable mesh sides provide clear sightlines from anywhere in the room, unlike bedside sleepers that are designed for only one viewing angle. Completely Freestanding – Unlike a bedside sleeper, which attaches to the bed, the Essential Bassinet is fully portable, making it easy to move around the house or take on the go. Easy Travel & Storage – Lightweight (15 lbs), packs flat, and includes a carry bag, making it ideal for traveling families or those with limited space. No Setup Restrictions – While bedside sleepers require a compatible bed height and attachment, the Essential Bassinet can be placed anywhere in the room with tool-free assembly.
