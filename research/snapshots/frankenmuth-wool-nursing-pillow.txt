The tiny pockets within the wool adapt your and your baby’s body temperature and allow air to circulate. This keeps you and baby comfortable, even in the hot summer months or during harsh winters. Pure & Sustainable Wool is a 100% renewable resource. Each year, sheep are sheared for their wool, a necessary part of ranching and sheep care. Rest assured, the sheep that grow your wool are still happily grazing California meadows. Healthy & Clean Our manufacturing process avoids the use of chemicals or synthetics, resulting in a product that is hypoallergenic, naturally flame-retardant, and resistant to dust mites, mildew, bacteria, and microbes. Wool Sourcing Our commitment to wool bedding quality and great sleep starts with the wool. We scoured the country to find ranchers who not only treat their sheep humanely but also share in our passion for providing materials and products that are made in the USA. Natural Processing Once the wool arrives at the Mill, it is processed traditionally using our century-old machinery. It's hand washed in bathtubs using 180-degree water and dried for 3 - 5 days, depending on the time of year. After the wool is dried, it is taken to our carding room, where it’s combed and processed on 100-year-old equipment using no chemicals or oils. After the wool is processed, it's taken to our finishing area, where 100% certified organic cotton nursing pillow coverings are sewn and made ready for wool filling. The wool filling is stuffed into the nursing pillow casing, and the edge is finished. Along each step through the Mill, there is great attention to detail so that each product we produce is delivered to our high standards. Comfort Matters With their small bodies, the temperature of a baby can quickly change depending on the temperature inside or outside.
