Our organic stuffed animals are roughly 12.6" x 12.6" x 6.7". Are these organic plush toys safe for babies? Yes, Naturepedic's organic stuffed animals are crafted from 100% certified organic cotton, making them a safer and healthier plush toy for babies. However, per Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) guidelines, it's important to note that soft objects, including stuffed animals, should not be placed in a baby's crib during sleep to reduce the risk of suffocation. Do these stuffed toys contain any plastic? No, these organic stuffed animals are made entirely from 100% certified organic cotton fabric and fill, with no plastic components or synthetic materials. Are these organic stuffed animals machine washable? No, these organic stuffed animals are not machine washable. To maintain their quality, we recommend spot cleaning with a damp cloth as needed.
