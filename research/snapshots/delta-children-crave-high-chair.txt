Description From first bites to family dinners for years to come, the Crave 4-in-1 Convertible High Chair by Delta Children is designed to grow with your family. Crafted with an FSC-certified beech wood frame, its clean, modern silhouette elevates any space while delivering long-lasting durability. This versatile design transitions seamlessly from high chair to toddler chair, child seat, and adult chair, supporting up to 310 pounds for years of everyday use. Thoughtfully engineered for comfort and safety, it features a wrap-around curved seatback, anti-slip rubber feet, and an innovative magnetic 5-point harness to help keep your child secure at every stage. Eight adjustable seat heights and seven footrest positions allow for a customized fit without the need for tools, ensuring proper support as your child grows. Cleanup is simple with wipeable silicone-coated straps, and a dishwasher-safe tray insert, while the lightweight frame and compact, self-standing fold make storage effortless, even in smaller spaces.
