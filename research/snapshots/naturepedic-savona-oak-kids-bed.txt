Go to item 1 Go to item 2 Go to item 3 Explore Sustainability Savona Kids Bed FAQs Is this bed made from solid wood? Yes. The Savona Kids Bed is made from solid white oak hardwood — not medium-density fiberboard or oriented strand board. Real-wood construction means genuine durability that holds up through every stage of childhood. What colors does this bed come in? The Savona is available in two finishes: Natural and Washed White. Both are coated with a water-based, low-VOC finish that meets food-grade standards. Is this bed safe for toddlers? The Savona Kids Bed is recommended for children ages 3 and up. It's a great first "big kid" bed, though it's not designed for toddlers transitioning directly from a crib. For bunk configurations, the top bunk is recommended for ages 6 and up, per safety guidelines. Is the bed durable enough for active kids? Built from solid white oak with a Janka hardness rating of 1360, the Savona is made to handle it all — sleepovers, pillow fights, and everything in between. Its solid frame provides long-lasting support through every stage of childhood. Can the bunk bed be separated into two individual beds? Yes. The Savona Bunk Bed can be separated into two standalone beds — no additional hardware needed. Note: in a Twin/Full configuration, the top and lower bunks will look different from each other when separated, as they have different headboard sizes. Can the Savona bunk bed be configured as a loft bed? No. The Savona is designed as a fixed two-bed configuration and cannot be converted to a loft. Can the ladder be attached to either side? Yes — the ladder can be installed on either side of the bunk bed, giving you flexibility to work with your room layout. Does the bed come with a trundle? The trundle is sold separately and can be added for extra sleeping space or storage. How do I clean and care for this bed? Dust regularly with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid direct sunlight and extreme temperatures. Blot spills immediately with a clean, damp cloth. Do not use chemical cleansers, abrasives, furniture polish, or wax. Periodically check and tighten all hardware connections to keep the frame sturdy over time. What is the weight limit? The maximum recommended weight is 400 lbs per sleeping surface — including the child and the mattress.
