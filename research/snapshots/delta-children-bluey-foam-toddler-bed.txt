Description Make the transition from a crib to a big-kid bed more exciting with the Bluey Foam Frame Toddler Bed by Delta Children. Designed for little Bluey fans, this toddler floor bed brings colorful character artwork and three-dimensional ears to a familiar bedtime routine. The low, ground-level profile makes it easy for toddlers to get in and out independently while helping reduce the risk of falls. The soft foam is in the frame and sides. The bottom is a thin fabric base with no foam padding or cushioning. The foam frame is not a mattress or a padded sleeping surface. This construction distinction helps families understand exactly what they are choosing before setting up their child's first big-kid bed. Unlike traditional wood or metal toddler beds, the foam frame has no hard frame edges. Built-in side rails help prevent your child from rolling out of the bed while keeping the low, accessible feel. Bluey graphics and three-dimensional ears bring character to the room, giving young fans a special place in their Bluey-themed bedroom. The soft foam sides form the surround, rather than cushioning the floor beneath your child. There is no assembly required. Simply remove the bed from its packaging and allow approximately 24-48 hours for the foam frame to fully expand before use. The cover is not removable, and the bed is not machine washable. Keep these construction and care details in mind when planning your child's bed setup. Designed for children ages 15 months and older, this toddler bed supports up to 50 lbs. and is Baby Safety Alliance Verified. Assembled dimensions are approximately 55.5"L x 32"W x 15.75"H, so you can plan its position in your child's bedroom before unpacking. Bluey by Delta Children combines BBC Studios' familiar character artwork, soft foam sides, built-in guardrails and a low-to-the-floor design.
