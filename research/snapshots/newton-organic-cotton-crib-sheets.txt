Certified organic crib sheets are those made from natural fibers that also satisfy strict social and environmental standards of softness and safety. That’s in sharp contrast to non-organic sheets, which are often made with synthetic chemicals. What does that mean for you and your baby? By choosing certified organic crib sheets, you can rest easy that your little one’s bedding is free from harmful chemicals and toxic dyes. It also means that the sheets will be super soft (while also being durable), comfortable, safe for their skin, and 100% breathable. Are Newton Crib Sheets organic? Every Newton Crib Sheet is made from 100% organic, super-soft muslin cotton that is just as breathable as our mattresses. Newton Crib Sheets provide a safe and gentle sleep experience and superior airflow for newborns and babies of all ages. Why breathable crib sheets? Newton’s breathable crib sheets are ideal for keeping your little one cozy, comfy, and safe overnight. The organic, super-soft muslin cotton allows for amazing breathability, which keeps your baby from getting too hot and helps to reduce the risk of suffocation. That makes for a longer, more restful night’s sleep — for both you and baby. What should I look for in crib sheets? When shopping for crib sheets, there are a few things to consider: Fit — Loose sheets can easily come off the corners of the mattress and endanger your little one while they sleep. Make sure the crib sheet you choose fits snugly on all four corners and doesn’t come loose when your baby moves. Care — You’ve got enough to do, so look for sheets that are easy to take care of and can be thrown into the washing machine with the rest of your baby’s clothes. Breathability — This serves two important purposes: to keep your baby safe (in case they turn over while they sleep) and to help them avoid overheating. Durability — Look for durable sheets that can withstand many washes without losing their structure. What size is a standard crib sheet? Standard crib sheets measure 52" x 28" and fit a crib mattress up to 8" thick. We designed our Newton crib sheets to fit securely over the Newton Essential, Original, and Waterproof Crib Mattresses, but they also fit securely on other mattresses of similar dimensions. For smaller cribs, try our mini version, which fits 24" x 38" mattresses, like Newton's Mini Crib Mattress. How many crib sheets do I need? You’ll only need one fitted crib sheet at a time — and nothing else in the crib until they’re older — but it’s a good idea to have extras on hand in case your baby has an accident, like a diaper blowout or throw-up from an upset stomach. Since these accidents can happen at any time (and often more than once in a single night), it’s best to be prepared.
