B Product Overview Previous Next Product total Options total Total Flat Woven Wool Rug quantity Add to Cart Product Details The Home of Wool flat-woven rug is handcrafted to order, using only pure wool in its natural hues of cream, grey, brown. With no dyes or treatments, the fibers remain undyed and completely chemical-free. It’s an ideal option for anyone creating a safe, hypoallergenic environment for their family. Wool naturally resists dust mites and bacteria, which makes your home healthier. It can even absorb VOCs that come from furniture or finishes, helping the air feel cleaner and fresher. Beyond these wellness benefits, a wool rug adds comfort you notice every day. It warms up the floor and gives a soft, cushioned feel under your feet. For cleaning advice, see our wool rug care guide . FAQs Is the rug treated with any chemicals? No, it is entirely chemical-free. It has no dyes or added chemicals. What colors are available? The Home of Wool rug comes in 4 color varieties: • Natural cream • Natural grey • Natural brown • Striped mix These are the genuine shades of wool itself. Pure and undyed. Can I choose fringe or no fringe? Yes. You can decide on that finishing detail. How should I clean it? Regular vacuuming is enough for everyday care. For spills, blot gently with a clean cloth. From time to time, shake the rug outside to refresh the fibers. For a deeper clean, professional wool cleaning is recommended. Can I order a custom size? Absolutely. Each rug is handcrafted in variety of dimensions so you can get the best fit for your room.
