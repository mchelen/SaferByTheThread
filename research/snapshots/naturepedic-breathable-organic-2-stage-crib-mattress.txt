Heather, Verified Buyer Previous Go to item 1 Go to item 2 Go to item 3 Go to item 4 Go to item 5 Next Breathable Organic Crib Mattress FAQs How can a mattress be breathable and waterproof? A crib mattress can be both breathable and waterproof when those features come from different layers working together. The removable breathable layer allows air to move through the surface where Baby rests, while the seamless waterproof surface underneath helps stop messes from reaching the mattress core. This approach keeps the sleep surface breathable for added airflow, while still making the mattress easy to wipe clean after leaks, spit-up, or accidents. Is this mattress less breathable than mattresses that are breathable all the way through? No. The breathable layer approach is designed to provide airflow where it matters most: directly at the sleep surface. In an independent suffocation-risk test using a simulated baby positioned face down (conducted by Intertek Labs), Naturepedic’s breathable layer scored in the lowest risk category, and the test found no added breathability benefit to making the entire mattress porous from top to bottom. That means the mattress can allow airflow through the surface while still keeping a seamless waterproof layer underneath to protect the core from moisture, contamination, and difficult-to-dry messes. This mattress feels uncomfortable! Why is it so firm? Crib mattresses are supposed to feel much firmer than adult mattresses. Babies need a firm, flat surface that helps prevent sinking, which is why Naturepedic crib mattresses follow CPSC and pediatrician recommendations for firm support and a snug fit. If it feels too firm to you, that's usually a good sign that it's designed appropriately for Baby. What size crib does this crib mattress fit? The Breathable Organic Crib Mattress fits standard size cribs and toddler beds. Its dimensions are approximately 27.25” x 51.625” x 6”, which is designed for standard U.S. cribs. Learn more on the blog: Will a Naturepedic Crib Mattress Fit Your Crib? How long can my baby use this crib mattress? Your baby can use this mattress from infancy through the toddler years, as long as it fits your crib or toddler bed and remains in good condition. If you choose the 2-stage option, the firmer side is designed for infants, while the slightly less firm side can be used once your child is around 1 year old. The weight limits for the Breathable Organic Crib Mattress are 80 lbs (lightweight) and 100 lbs (innerspring). How do I clean this crib mattress? When accidents happen (or for everyday maintenance), remove and wash the breathable cover. The mattress has a wipe-clean waterproof surface, so most messes can be cleaned with water and/or mild soap. The heat-sealed waterproof seams help prevent liquids from seeping in along the sides, making cleanup simple from edge to edge. When should I flip the two-sided crib mattress? Use the firmer side for infants. Once your baby is approximately 1 year old, you can flip the 2-stage mattress to the slightly less firm toddler side for a more cushioned feel. Both sides are still designed to meet crib mattress firmness recommendations. What is the difference between lightweight and innerspring? The lightweight option uses Naturepedic's patented WaveSupport™ core, which is strong, supportive, and easier to lift for sheet changes. The innerspring option uses a 150-coil steel system with a heavy-duty border rod for added edge support and a more traditional mattress construction. Both are firm and durable, so the choice mostly comes down to whether you prefer easier lifting or a more classic innerspring feel. If you plan on using the crib mattress for multiple children, we recommend the innerspring mattress.
