Make diaper changes cleaner, safer and more comfortable with the Delta Children Molded Waterproof Changing Pad. Its supportive molded construction creates a soft, cushioned surface for your baby, while the gently angled design helps direct accidents downward and away from your little one, making changing your baby easier than ever.
