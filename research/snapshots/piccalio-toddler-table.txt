Made of real wood Multiple ways to use Easy to clean Frequently Asked Questions See all FAQs What is a Montessori table? A Montessori table is a child-sized table, typically made of natural wood. Dr. Montessori was one of the first people to create child-sized furniture. She noticed children benefit from being able to arrange and use furniture independently. To use a Montessori table and chair the Montessori way, show your child how to pull out and push in the chair. Also, encourage your child to care for their space by putting art supplies, dishes, and/or toys away after using the table. How does the Piccalio Play Table grow with a child over time? Unlike many toddler-only tables, this design is intentionally simple and solid enough to evolve with your child’s needs. It begins as a weaning or activity table, transitions into an art and building station, and can later function as a side table or nightstand. Families often choose it because it supports independence early on while remaining useful well beyond the toddler years. Are weaning tables worth it? A weaning table is a small table designed for little ones who are starting to eat solid foods. An alternative to a high chair, typically, children transfer to a weaning table around 12 to 18 months. A weaning table can be worth it for many families, as this set-up fosters independence and facilitates peaceful mealtimes. Any toddler table and chair set can serve as a weaning table. Our Reversible Chair and Table Set features a chair with 3 height options so it grows with your child. The lowest height is ideal for 1 to 2-year-olds, making it an excellent weaning table option. When to introduce play table? Toddlers can begin using a table around the time they can walk steadily. For most children, this is between 12 and 18 months of age. Getting your child a toddler table can boost independence and confidence. Toddlers often like to stand as they color, read, and play. Your child can stand at the toddler play table as they work. Alternatively, provide your child with a complete Chair and Play Table set . Toddlers love a chair just their size and can build essential gross motor skills as they pull out the chair and practice sitting. Is this table suitable for Montessori-style independent work? Yes. Montessori environments emphasize appropriately sized furniture that allows children to sit, stand, and move independently. The Play Table’s proportions are designed to support self-directed activities like drawing, snack preparation, puzzles, and practical life work. Its weight and stability help prevent tipping, encouraging confidence during independent use. Is Piccalio’s Play Table durable enough for rough daily use? The table is crafted from solid European beech wood with reinforced joinery and a protective non-toxic finish. Unlike lightweight composite or veneer pieces, solid wood construction holds up to repeated leaning, art projects, climbing attempts, and everyday wear. Material quality and construction methods are often what separate long-lasting furniture from short-term alternatives. How do I assemble a Piccalio product? Montessori-inspired spaces prioritize real materials and appropriately sized furniture that supports independence. Our solid wood Play Table offers stability, tactile feedback, and longevity that many lightweight plastic options lack.
