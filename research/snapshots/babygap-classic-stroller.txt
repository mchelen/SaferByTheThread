Designed for the future in every possible way, all the soft fabrics on the stroller are made from recycled water bottles. Plastic drinking bottles are sorted, cleaned and shredded into flakes that become the yarn the seat fabric is made from. A great choice for your child and the planet, this everyday stroller is outfitted with front wheels that features precision technology for outstanding control and effortless push, making it easier than ever to glide, turn and maneuver any surface. Incredibly cozy, the stroller has an extra-large extendable canopy with sun visor, reclining seat and tons of storage. The extra-large storage basket and removable parent organizer ensure there’s always enough room for all your baby’s on-the-go essentials. Ideal for travel or everyday use, the stroller’s durable steel frame only weighs 14.5 pounds and the compact fold, auto lock and carry handle make it easy to transport. Uniquely babyGap, the stroller has the easily recognized babyGap logo and iconic Brannan Bear on the wheels, canopy, calf rest, parent organizer and safety harness for an elevated look. This stroller is recommended for infants 9 months up to 50 pounds.
