full details Reversible Climbing Ramp $149.00 Add to Cart Description Dimensions Materials Shipping Info FAQ The RAD Reversible Climbing Ramp is a versatile movement piece designed to grow with your child and expand the possibilities of RAD climbing structures. Designed to complement RAD Pikler Triangles, the Climbing Arch, and the Play Cube, it offers both gentle introductions to climbing and more advanced challenges as children gain confidence. For infants and early climbers, the ramp can be placed on a low rung to create a shallow incline, making it an inviting first experience with climbing structures. As children grow stronger and more adventurous, the ramp can be positioned higher to function as a slide or a steeper climbing challenge. The ramp features two usable sides: one smooth side for sliding and controlled movement, and one side with rungs for climbing and grip practice. Children naturally adjust the angle and orientation based on their comfort level, allowing play to remain child led and adaptable. An additional stabilizing rung helps prevent pivoting when the ramp is in use. Handmade in our shop from premium Baltic birch wood, the Reversible Climbing Ramp reflects RAD’s commitment to sturdy construction, thoughtful design, and quality materials. It supports children up to 100 pounds and features smooth, easy to clean surfaces for everyday use. While the ramp works well on its own, it truly shines as an add on to RAD climbing pieces. It can also be laid flat between two structures to create a balance beam or bridge, expanding opportunities for coordination and imaginative play.
