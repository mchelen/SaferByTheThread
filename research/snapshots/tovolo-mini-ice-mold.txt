Questions, answered Are Tovolo ice molds dishwasher safe? Yes. The silicone molds and trays go straight in the dishwasher. A quick rinse with water and white vinegar also clears any mineral film. How long does the ice take to freeze? Plan on freezing overnight for large cubes, spheres and shaped ice, so they are solid all the way through. What else can I freeze in them? Coffee for iced coffee, juice for smoothies, herbs in oil for cooking, and single portions of sauce or baby food. What are the molds made of? BPA free, food grade silicone that stays flexible through freeze after freeze without turning brittle or flavoring your ice. Why is my ice cloudy? Cloudy ice is trapped air and minerals, which every standard mold produces. It melts just as slowly as clear ice. For clearer results, start with boiled or filtered water that has cooled. My black silicone has a white film. Is it damaged? No. That film is mineral residue from hard water and dishwasher detergent. Wipe it with white vinegar or soak in warm water and vinegar, then rinse. My silicone smells like garlic or soap. How do I fix it? Scrub with a paste of baking soda and water, rinse, and let it dry fully. For stubborn odors, a short soak in diluted white vinegar helps.
