No wonder comfort is one of the must-haves for making those nursing hours easier and more enjoyable. Our handcrafted U-shaped nursing pillow is designed to ease the strain on your arms, shoulders, and back. This way, you can focus on what matters most. Shaped in a soft U, the pillow rests securely around you and helps your baby stay in a natural feeding position, providing the needed breastfeeding support. The wool filling provides steady lift, helping position your baby for easier latching. Wool keeps you comfortable and dry. Also, never too hot. Add a removable cover or pillowcase for easy care . You can also include a personal embroidery, which makes the pillow a thoughtful gift and a beautiful keepsake. Truly versatile, this nursing pillow can continue to serve as a cozy support for tummy time and helping your baby practice sitting with support. The supportive design of the Home of Wool nursing pillow helps position your baby securely. It reduces strain and creates comfort whether you breastfeed or bottle-feed. What materials are used?
