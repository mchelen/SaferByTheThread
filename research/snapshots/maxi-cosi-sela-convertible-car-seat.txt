Our premium all-in-one car seat for babies, toddlers, and big kids focuses on comfort for your child and convenience for you. Sela features ClipQuik, our magnetic chest clip you can open 1-handed to make buckling and unbuckling faster. Plus, the Out-of-the-Way harness system helps eliminate fumbling with straps and buckles. Sela handles your child’s growth spurts with ease with QuikFit, our 1-hand, no-rethread integrated headrest and harness system that easily raises for the proper, comfortable fit. This grows with me car seat takes the worry out of installation with the Rigid LATCH connectors and built-in vehicle belt lock-off arm in both rear- and forward-facing positions that helps to get a secure fit easily and quickly. For added peace of mind, Sela has red-to-green indicators on the seat’s base allow you to see when your base is secured with the Rigid LATCH connectors. Key to the design of this infant to toddler car seat are the premium fabrics. The Sela all in one car seat is designed with EcoCare, our future-friendly, 100%-recycled fabric made from plastic bottles. The yarn produced is soft, comfortable, and breathable––perfect for your little one. And our soft, plush PureCosi TM fabric is made without added fire retardant treatments, which is better for both your child and our planet. All fabrics are easy to remove and machine-washable and dryer-safe. Stored in the tether door at the back of the seat, our seat pad tool helps tuck in the edges of the seat pad around the edges of the car seat. Our slim all-in-one car seat design fits 3 carseats across the backseat in most vehicles and has 3 modes: rear facing car seat (5–40 lbs., 19"–40"), forward facing car seat (30–65 lbs., 34.4"–49"), belt-positioning booster car seat (40–100 lbs., 43.4"–52").
