For example: – Do not place glassware directly from freezer to hot water and/or microwave. – Do not place hot glassware on a wet or cool surface. Allow glassware to cool before washing, refrigerating, or freezing. Do not microwave lids or accessories. Avoid handling hot glassware without potholders. Avoid microwave misuse (always use minimum amount of cooking time and never heat empty or near empty glassware.) Be careful when handling any broken glass because pieces may be sharp. Inspect glassware before every use. Never use or repair any glassware that is broken, chipped, cracked, etched, or severely scratched. Do not drop or hit glassware against hard objects or utensils. Be careful with placement of glassware in dishwasher to avoid contact with hard or sharp items during the wash cycle.
