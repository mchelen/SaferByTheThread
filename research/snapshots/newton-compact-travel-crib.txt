Close Top Compact Travel Crib & Play Yard Questions What is a Wovenaire Core Mattress? The Wovenaire Core Mattress is Newton’s patented ultra-breathable mattress, which has been proven to reduce the risk of suffocation and overheating. Not only is it safe, but our mattress is also comfortable and firm, supporting your little one’s body as they sleep. The Newton Wovenaire Core Mattress is also 100% washable, which means it’s a top choice for children with allergies or asthma. The mattress is made of 90% air and 10% recyclable food-grade polymer and is GREENGUARD Gold Certified. How long can I use this travel crib for my baby? The Compact Travel Crib is suitable for babies and toddlers up to 35” tall. When you’re on the go and you don’t know if a space is baby-proofed, the play yard is the perfect spot for safe play. With our travel crib and play yard, you don’t have to worry about electrical outlets, small objects in the room that could pose choking hazards (a worry for children under three years), or sharp corners. How do I clean the Wovenaire Core Mattress? Cleaning the Compact Travel Crib’s Wovenaire Core Mattress is easy. Unzip the outer cover and remove it from the core. Zip the cover closed and then wash it on a cold setting, choosing a delicate cycle. Tumble dry on low. Be sure not to bleach the cover, and replace it if it gets damaged. If you need to clean the core itself, you can wash it in the shower with soap and cool water or outside with a hose. After cleaning it, shake out the excess water and let it stand to dry in a well-ventilated area. Be sure to avoid direct sunlight, and don’t use any heating implements to try to dry it faster.
