Learn More We're here to help Personal Support, Every Step of the Way Read All FAQs Why choose a wool mattress protector instead of a synthetic one? Obasan’s virgin wool is naturally breathable, eco-friendly, and free of harmful chemicals. Unlike synthetic protectors, it regulates temperature, absorbs moisture, and creates a natural barrier against dust mites, ensuring a healthier, more comfortable sleep environment. Does a wool mattress protector make the bed warmer? No. Wool is a thermoregulating fiber, it retains warmth in winter while staying cool in summer. It adapts to your body and the room’s temperature, offering comfort all year round. Is it compatible with all types of mattresses? Yes, our wool protector is designed to fit Obasan mattresses perfectly, including our customizable models. It also works with other mattresses, as long as the sizing matches. How often should I wash it? Wool naturally resists odors and wicks away moisture, so it doesn’t need frequent washing, regular airing is usually enough. When cleaning is needed, it’s important to know that wool does not react well to heat. Please refer to our full care instructions: hand wash in cold tap water, or use the gentle cycle on your washing machine with cold water only. Always hang to dry indoors, away from direct heat or sunlight. Can I use it with a mattress topper? Absolutely. You can place the protector directly on the mattress or over a topper, depending on the type. Combined, they add a plush feel to a firm mattress while maintaining excellent breathability. Is it safe for children? Yes. It’s hypoallergenic and free from harmful substances. The baby size is specifically designed without elastic straps for added safety, making it ideal for babies and sensitive sleepers alike.
