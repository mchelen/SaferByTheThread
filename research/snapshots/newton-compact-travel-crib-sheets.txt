Machine wash cold with like colors. Do not bleach. Tumble dry low. Do not iron. Do not dry clean. FAQ's Will this fit the Play Yard Bassinet Insert or just the floor pad? This sheet was designed to pair perfectly with the floor pad of the Travel Crib & Play Yard. We do not offer Bassinet Insert Extra Sheets at this time. Do I have to use a sheet? While the pad is completely washable, we recommend using the specialty sheet designed for the Travel Crib & Play Yard floor pad. It’s super breathable, soft, and easy to launder when needed, reducing the need to wash the entire pad. Can I use different sheets? Our play yard is designed as the largest in the industry, featuring the maximum thickness available for a play yard mat for extra comfort and support. Because of this, the Newton Play Yard Floor Mat sheets are specific to the product and are the only sheets that should be used with our play yard. Newton Baby’s breathable sheets are ideal for keeping your little one cozy, comfy, and safe day and night. The super-soft muslin cotton allows for amazing breathability, which keeps your baby from overheating and helps to reduce the risk of suffocation. That makes for a longer, more restful sleep — for both you and baby. How many sheets do I need? First and foremost, remember that you only need one fitted sheet at a time on your baby’s mattress and nothing else — no top sheet, blanket, or pillow. Since accidents can (and will!) happen when you least expect them, it’s always best to be prepared. We recommend having several sheets on hand to continue providing your little one with the comfort and safety they need.
