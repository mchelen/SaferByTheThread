Designed with your kid’s health and safety in mind, this organic baby pillow is completely hypoallergenic. This is due to the fact that wool naturally resists dust mites and bacteria. It’s great for infants and babies with allergies or sensitivity to chemicals. Entirely free from synthetics, dyes, chemical treatments, off-gassing, and VOCs, ensuring a clean and breathable sleep environment at home. The lowered center gently cradles the head and is commonly used by parents as an anti-flat head pillow. The side zipper is great for easily adjusting the wool filling inside. Handcrafted with care and made-to-order, it is designed for sustainability. The organic baby pillow is 100% biodegradable and made exclusively from renewable, nature-friendly materials. FAQs Is a wool baby pillow safe for infants? Natural and treated with no chemicals, the Home of Wool baby pillow is the safest alternative. What are the benefits of wool? It is breathable and is also resistant to dust mites and mold. It is the best hypoallergenic option. Can I change the thickness or firmness of the pillow? Yes! A zipper lets you adjust the filling of the baby pillow. Is this pillow chemical-free? No flame retardants. No chemical treatments. No off-gassing. How should I clean the pillow? Use a washable pillowcase. Avoid washing the pillow with the filling. Spot clean with natural cleaners. Air out in the sun to refresh. Will this pillow help prevent flat head syndrome? Many parents use it to help prevent or correct plagiocephaly. Always follow your pediatrician’s guidance on safe sleep practices and healthy development. Is it suitable for babies with asthma? Yes! Wool is ideal for asthma, and our organic baby pillow offers all its hypoallergenic benefits. It repels dust mites, one of the most common triggers of asthma flare-ups. What materials are used in the pillow? It is filled with pure wool and covered in cotton. The optional pillowcases are made from natural undyed fabrics as well.
