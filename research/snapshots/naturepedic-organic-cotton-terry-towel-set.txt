Go to item 1 Go to item 2 Go to item 3 Go to item 4 Explore Sustainability Organic Bath Towel Set FAQs What is cotton terry fabric? Terry is a style of weave that uses thousands of tiny loops to increase surface area. Those loops make the fabric exceptionally absorbent while giving it a soft, plush feel that's ideal for bath towels. What is included in the 6-piece towel set? Each set comes with two plush bath towels (56" x 30"), two hand towels (28" x 16"), and two washcloths (12" x 12") — everything you need for a complete refresh. What makes these luxury bath towels sustainable? They're made from 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton, grown without synthetic pesticides or fertilizers, and processed to strict environmental standards per the Global Organic Textile Standard. Choosing organic helps to support healthier soil, cleaner waterways, and more responsible textile production. Are organic bath towels safe for sensitive skin? Yes. Organic cotton avoids unnecessary chemical finishes or irritants, making it a gentle choice for all skin types, including babies and those with sensitivities. Do these terry towels stay fluffy after laundering? With proper care, yes. Washing in cold water and tumble-drying on low helps the terry loops maintain their structure, so the towels stay soft, absorbent, and fluffy wash after wash.
