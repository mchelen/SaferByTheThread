This bento box also grows with your child, offering a removable insert to allow for a larger compartment if needed. Designed for Kids – With two, easy-to-open latches, young kids can learn to open their lunch independently, and feel confident while doing so! In addition, its compact size allows them to carry it to and from school or day care in their backpack (recommended for ages 3-8). NOTE: The 2-compartment insert is not leakproof – use this for dry snacks only!
