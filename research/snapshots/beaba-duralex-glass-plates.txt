Created in collaboration with iconic French glassware brand DURALEX and made in France, this plate set is crafted from tempered glass that's 2.5 times stronger than standard glass. Age: 4mo - 36mo+ Why You'll Love It About Duralex Since 1945, DURALEX has been the pioneer in tempered glass technology, and are the specialists in this process. Their factory in La Chapelle-Saint-Mesmin was the first to produce glass tempered tableware in the world. Duralex is unquestionably renowned as the leader of tempered glass. Their glass features improved physical qualities: it is thermal shock (sudden temperature change) resistant and 2.5X more resistant than non-tempered glass, thus making a great non-toxic feeding material for babies and toddlers alike!
