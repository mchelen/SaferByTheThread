No, not all changing table pads are the same size. Changing table pad dimensions can vary by brand and style, so it’s important to check both the size of your changing table and the dimensions of the pad before purchasing. Naturepedic’s organic changing table pad is designed to fit many standard changing tables and dressers. How big is this changing table pad? Naturepedic’s organic changing pad measures approximately 16.5" x 31.5" x 4". This size is designed to give Baby a comfortable changing area while still fitting many standard changing tables and dressers. Always measure your nursery furniture first to confirm the best fit. What’s the difference between a two-sided and four-sided changing pad? The two-sided changing table pad has raised sides on the left and right, giving Baby more room to grow vertically while still offering a contoured changing surface. The four-sided organic changing table pad has raised edges on all four sides for a more enclosed feel. Both options are designed to provide firm, comfortable support for everyday diaper changes. How do I secure the changing pad to the table? This organic diaper changing pad includes snap screws to help secure the pad to a dresser or changing table. It also features a built-in safety buckle to help keep Baby more supported during changes. Always follow the setup instructions and keep one hand on Baby during every diaper change. How do I clean this changing table pad? The waterproof surface is designed to wipe clean after diaper leaks, spit-up, diaper cream spills, and everyday messes. Simply wipe the organic cotton changing pad surface as needed, then let it dry before the next use. If you use the optional fitted cover, remove and launder it as needed according to the care instructions. Can I purchase additional covers? Yes, an optional fitted cover is available for this organic cotton changing pad. It adds an extra layer of softness where Baby rests and can be removed for laundering. Having an additional cover on hand can make it easier to keep your changing area fresh between washes.
