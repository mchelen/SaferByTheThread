Description Make the transition from crib to their first big-kid bed feel safe, comfortable and exciting with the Finley Metal Toddler Bed with Spindles by Delta Children. Designed especially for growing toddlers, its low-profile mattress position keeps sleep closer to the floor to help minimize fall risk, while built-in safety side rails provide added security throughout the night. The low-to-the-ground design also follows Montessori principles by giving little ones easy, independent access to their bed, helping build confidence as they settle into a new sleep routine. Crafted with a timeless metal frame and classic spindle detailing, the Finley adds an elevated look to any toddler room while maintaining a compact footprint that fits easily into smaller spaces. It accommodates any standard-size crib mattress, so parents can easily reuse their existing mattress when transitioning out of the crib. Baby Safety Alliance Verified for added peace of mind, the Finley Metal Toddler Bed is recommended for children ages 15 months and older and supports up to 50 lbs.
