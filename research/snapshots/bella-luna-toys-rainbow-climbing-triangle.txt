Hand-crafted from maple hardwood with the utmost care and attention to detail, our exclusive play triangle will become a treasured addition to all play spaces to encourage kinetic movement, play, and exploration. This extremely durable, safe, and long-lasting climbing toy encourages children to explore their world in creative ways and is 100% powered by imagination to promote open-ended play. Inspired by Hungarian pediatrician and early childhood educator, Emmi Pikler and used in many Montessori and Waldorf classrooms, this folding indoor climbing frame helps babies, toddlers, and older children develop gross motor skills as they learn to climb. This rainbow colored climbing triangle is not just for climbing but will bring joy as a hideout, a tunnel, a mountain, a seat, and so much more! At Bella Luna Toys, we believe that by incorporating movement into play, children are inspired into deep levels of creativity and learning. Easily folded for storage, when open the frame is designed to be self-supporting. Simply remove the locking pins to fold the frame and re-insert the locking pins to lock closed. Once locked closed, the triangle can be stored anywhere, whether under the couch, bed or hanging behind a door. Add even more open-ended play possibilities to your child's Climbing Triangle with a Wooden Ladder Accessory!
