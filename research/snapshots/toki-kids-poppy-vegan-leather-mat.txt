A portion of our proceeds goes to Every Mother Counts: Providing Maternity Care Worldwide Learn More Play Mat FAQ What is the play mat made of? Our play mats consist of one fabric cover with two side zip openings, and two natural latex foam inserts. Our play mat cover options are 100% organic cotton, vegan leather, and a blend of bamboo jersey + organic cotton + spandex. The cotton covers are GOTS-certified organic, and the vegan leather and bamboo jersey blend covers are OEKO-TEX certified. Our play mat foam inserts are 1” of 100% natural Dunlop latex foam and Oeko-Tex certified. The waterproof cover sets are optional, and come as a set of two zippered covers. The waterproof covers are 100% bamboo jersey fabric and Oeko-Tex certified. What is on the underside of the mat? The bottom of the outer cover is made with the same fabric as the top (either 100% cotton or vegan leather depending on mat type). Unlike the top, the bottom has a crease down the middle of the mat where the two foam pieces meet. Is latex foam safe? Latex foam is a natural foam rubber that's manufactured using sap from the rubber tree. It is 100% natural, non-toxic, untreated with flame retardants, anti-bacterial, self-ventilating, biodegradable, and non-allergenic. It is the perfect consistency for a soft but firm play surface. You can read more about latex foam here . Because our latex foam is made without any synthetic chemicals, the final product often has slight variations in appearance that do not affect quality, performance, or durability in any way. It is common for small tears and bubbles to form during the baking process, and any discolorations you may notice are the residuals of burnt latex sap. These imperfections are the natural result of clean manufacturing, and are undetectable once the foam is inserted into the play mat cover. Is the play mat safe for my child to sleep on? Although the materials are safe, this specific product is not intended for sleep. The play mat is 100% breathable and self-ventilating, but it has not been treated with flame retardants as is required by CPSC-regulated sleep products. As a reminder, if your child does take a nap, you should not leave your child unattended sleeping on the mat. How do I clean my mat? Vegan leather cover: Our vegan leather mat can be wiped clean with any chemical-free cleaner. Foam inserts: Natural latex foam is not water resistant. Please do not attempt to wash it, but instead spot clean with mild soap and water. We also offer add-on waterproof cover sets to help protect against spills. In the case that you need a foam replacement, please check our product listings. If you need just one foam insert replaced instead of the whole set, please reach out to support@tokikids.com What is vegan leather made from? Our vegan leather is crafted from Oeko-Tex 100 certified, water-based, microfiber PU leather. Can I use my vegan leather mat outside? Yes, our vegan leather cover is waterproof and can be used both indoors or outdoors. Although the cover can get wet and be wiped clean, exposure to water for a prolonged period of time can cause the cover to become damaged. Make sure to bring your play mat indoors or keep it covered during rainfall. How do I reassemble my mat? Follow these video instructions to reassemble your play mat after washing the cover! What are the dimensions of the play mat when it is folded? Our standard mat measures around 20 x 20 x 6 when folded, our mega mat measures around 30 x 20 x 6 when folded, and the epic mat measures around 36 x 20 x 12 when folded. How much does the play mat weigh? Our standard mat weighs around 9 lbs, our mega mat weighs around 18 lbs, and our epic mat weighs around 24 lbs.
