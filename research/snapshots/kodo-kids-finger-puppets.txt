We offer five themed (African, Farm, Garden, Woodland, and Sheep) groups of four puppets. Each themed group can be used to support songs, finger plays, story telling, and story making. What's Included The African themed group includes a giraffe, a lion, an elephant and a zebra puppet. The Garden themed group includes a butterfly, a bee, a flower and a fairy puppet. The Woodland themed group includes a fox, a wolf, a raccoon and a badger puppet. The Farm themed group includes a sheep, a horse, a chicken and a cow puppet. The Sheep group includes two white sheep and two black sheep. Dimensions Puppets measure approximately 1.5" wide and 3-4" tall. Care Instructions Clean wool puppets by spot cleaning or submerge in mild soapy water, rinse thoroughly and air dry. Hand washable only.
