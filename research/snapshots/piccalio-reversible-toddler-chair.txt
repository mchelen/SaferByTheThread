Frequently Asked Questions See all FAQs What is a Montessori chair? A Montessori chair is a child-sized chair, typically made of natural wood. Dr. Montessori was one of the first people to create child-sized furniture. She noticed children benefit from being able to arrange and use furniture independently. To teach your child to use their chair the Montessori way, show them how to safely move the chair where they want to sit. Our toddler chair is perfect for reading books, sitting at a table, or putting on shoes. What chair should a 2 year old be in? A 2 year old should sit in a child-sized chair. For optimal sitting position, your child’s hips, knees, and ankles should all bend at a 90 degree angle. This ensures your child is comfortable and also promotes good posture.
