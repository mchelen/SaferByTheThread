Balance Beam is an open ended movement piece designed to support balance, coordination, and imaginative play for confident walkers and older children. Ideal for home play spaces and Montessori classrooms, this versatile set invites children to move, explore, and create their own physical challenges. The Balance Beam includes four beam sections and five connector blocks, allowing you to build a balance path that extends over nine feet long. Each 28 inch section can be arranged into straight lines, zigzags, or creative shapes, encouraging children to experiment with movement and problem solving. Additional sets can be added to expand the beam even further as your child’s confidence and creativity grow. Children use the Balance Beam in many ways. It may become a path to walk, a toy ramp, a boundary for imaginative play, or a catwalk for dolls and figures. The low profile design keeps play approachable while still offering meaningful opportunities to practice balance and body awareness. Handmade in our shop from premium Baltic birch wood, the Balance Beam reflects RAD’s commitment to thoughtful design, quality materials, and sturdy construction. Despite its lightweight build, the full set supports at least 200 pounds, making it suitable for shared play and long term use.
