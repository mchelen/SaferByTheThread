Full name Email Phone Number --> Email address Sign up Kids' Rocking Chair for Gross Motor and Emotional Regulation Encourage movement and mindfulness with the Whale Rocking Chair. Designed to support gross motor development and emotional regulation through play, this child-sized rocker offers open-ended opportunities for kids to build strength, coordination, and balance. The gentle, repetitive motion is work known as heavy work, engaging the proprioceptive and vestibular systems. As children rock, they strengthen muscles, improve spatial awareness, and experience a calming sensory rhythm that helps them regulate emotions and focus. Features Sturdy design featuring toolless assembly and disassembly Foot rest and handles Can be stored standing up on its tail, or can be disassembled to pack flat for storage under the bed or in the closet.
