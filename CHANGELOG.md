# Changelog

All notable changes to the site are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- Filters on result pages (departments, product types, materials, brands, search, the shop) sit in one row of chips above the results, as on Amazon: Filters (the full panel), Product type, Age range, Brand, Materials, Tests, Price, Shipping, Ships from and a Free shipping toggle. Each chip opens its options in a dropdown (a sheet from the bottom on phones); the Materials dropdown is the same tree, with the same checked-is-included rules. A chip with something on shows filled, with its choice or a count, and Clear all appears at the end. The row scrolls sideways and stays under the header on phones. The left filter rail, the quick-filter row and the row of removable filter chips are gone, so results use the full width (five across on desktop).

- Materials shown as icons: cards and the product page's buy box show one chip per material (an icon and a short name, the same materials as the Materials filter), a Synthetic chip whenever a part is plastic or synthetic, a Flagged chip for other flagged parts, and a chip counting the parts no page states. - Changing a filter no longer makes the page jump: the window and the filter rail keep their scroll position, facets left on "Show all" stay expanded, and a shorter list keeps enough room to hold the spot (the empty moment while the list was rebuilt let the browser snap to the top).

### Changed
- Made with and Leave out are one **Materials** tree: a checked material or kind is included, an unchecked one is left out, and a product shows when it has none of what was left out and at least one of what was kept. Families (Natural fibres, Plastic and synthetics, Added treatments) open into their members; a material opens into the kinds of it to leave out (Wood > Plywood, MDF or veneer; Silicone > Not stated as tested to LFGB). A parent is checked when it and all its children are, part-checked when some are, unchecked when left out (its children then show unchecked); checking a child includes its material. "Only" beside a material or family keeps just that one; `/shop?mat=wood` is wood only. Chips read "No plywood, MDF or veneer", "Only wood", "Every part’s material stated". Defaults are unchanged.
- Material filters regrouped and nested into three groups: **Made with** (pick materials, any of them; Natural fibres opens into wool, cotton, linen and silk; a material opens into the filters about it, such as Silicone > Only tested to LFGB or the French Arrêté, Wood > Only solid wood, Wool > Only untreated wool), **Leave out** (Plastic and synthetics and Chemical treatments, each one checkbox that opens into its parts, plus "Parts whose material isn’t stated"), and **Tests & certifications** (the separate Standards named filter folded in; standards a test filter already covers are left out). Labels lose the repeated "No", help lines under each filter are gone (the ? popover stays), chips read "Leave out: …", "Made with: …" or "Silicone: …", and the product page By-filter table uses the same three headings. Plastic is no longer offered as a Made with choice.
- Made with lists only parts whose material the page names. 26 lines on 16 listings that named a part by construction alone ("Fleece lining", "Padding, 300 g/m²", "Frame (material not stated)") now read under Not stated as the fibre or material no page gives; `npm run check` rejects such lines under `uses`. A "Fleece (fabric)" glossary entry explains that fleece names a knit, not a fibre, and "lining" no longer opens the hidden-parts entry. The Strollers type intro says how footmuff pages describe materials.
- Remarks about testing or certificates ("LFGB certificate not published", "No regulatory claim on the page") on 9 silicone tray and mould listings now read as what is not stated, under Not stated, instead of as flagged parts; "thin felt, not padded" on two play mats and a Que Choisir note on a Lékué mould moved to the listing note. `npm run check` rejects such remarks under `outside`.
- Listing pages show every material filter that is on as a chip above the results, defaults included, each with its own remove button; the product page's "By filter" table lists only the filters that are on, instead of every filter with an "Off" tag.
- Search result cards and quick view no longer show a line when a product meets your filters with nothing unstated, since every result meets them; only "Not stated" lines remain. The compare table says "Meets them all".
- "Every detail stated" now reads "All materials stated" on cards, quick view and the compare table; the filter "Every detail above is stated" is "Materials stated for every part", and the sort "Fewest details not stated" is "Fewest unstated materials".
- "Not stated" lines on cards, quick view, the compare table and product pages name the parts no page describes ("Not stated: thread · zipper · wool treatment"), from a new `unstated` field that ties each unconfirmed part to its material filter (170 parts tagged).
- Fourteen listings recorded a guess as fact ("probably plastic", "likely synthetic", "often polyester"); each guessed part is now listed as not stated, so 72 products meet the default filters instead of 64. General context about dyes moved to the silks and pretend play intros. `npm run check` now rejects guessing words in the checklist.
- Type photo tiles with no listing photo yet show the type's initial on the plate instead of an empty circle.
- Picture navigation on the remaining pages: the shop lists every product type as a photo tile under its title, search results show the types that have matches, a product page ends with "More in <category>" type tiles, and empty Saved, Compare and not-found pages show the category tiles instead of a lone button.
- Product-type pages show the other types in their category as photo tiles under the title, with the current one marked; the brands directory shows a product photo next to each brand; type suggestions in the search box carry the type's photo.
- Home page navigates by pictures: category tiles, age chips and a row of round product-type photo tiles under each category, in place of text lists of product types; the explanatory sections (what every listing tells you, your material filters) moved off the home page, which keeps one line of copy under the title. The featured rail no longer squeezes its first cards on phones and tablets.

The menu and department pages show product types as photo tiles with short names; old aisle links (`/a/play`, `/a/sleep`, `/a/eat`, `/a/wear`) redirect.
- Cards, quick view and the comparison table name which material filter a product misses or can't be judged on ("Not stated: Small parts +1") instead of counting details; the product page leads with the same lines, hides empty checklist rows and explanatory subtitles, and groups the "By filter" table like the filter sidebar with switched-off filters marked. Options in `docs/materials-at-a-glance.md`.
- Store buttons are the main action: a large amber "Buy at <store>" button right under the price on product pages and in quick view, a "Buy at store" button on every product card and in the Compare table, and the same button in the phone buy bar.

The home page leads with product types. `data.json` moved to schema 4 (flat `entries`, `types`, `aisles`); `/d/<department>` and `/c/pads` links redirect. "Pads, toppers, pillows and sheets" split into three types.

- Site renamed "Saved by the Thread" with the tagline "Reading the fine print so you don't have to." (#1)

### Removed
- The "Leave out these materials" filters ("No natural rubber or latex", "No leather, suede or sheepskin"). They were the reverse of the Made with options for the same materials, which is where a visitor picks them.
- The Python single-page build (`build.py`, `template.html`); the site now renders from `data.json` in the browser. (#1)
