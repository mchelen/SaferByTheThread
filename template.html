How to read a card

  • ✓Made with: materials the maker or store names.
  • ⊘Stated free of: things the maker says are absent.
  • ?Not stated: parts no page describes, such as thread, glue or dye.
  • ✕Flagged parts: parts a filter can leave out, such as plastic, synthetic fill, an unnamed colourant or treated wool.

A card covers one product, or a brand's line of products made the same way. Its label says whether it meets your filters or which filters it misses. Items that miss are kept in a closed list under each product type. The guide reports what makers and stores say and doesn't rank products by safety or risk.{{GLOSSARY_LINK}}
